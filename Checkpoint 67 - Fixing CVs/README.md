## 5 Resume Mistakes & How They Were Fixed

* **Headshot Photo & Multi-Column Layout:** Removed the profile photo and switched from a two-column sidebar to a clean single-column layout so Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) can parse the text accurately.
* **Vague, Unquantified Bullet Points:** Replaced filler phrases like *"so many customer satisfaction rating"* and *"by a high rate"* with measurable results (e.g., *"Maintained a 95%+ customer satisfaction rating"* and *"Boosted team delivery efficiency by 30%"*).
* **Broken LinkedIn URL & Placeholder Contacts:** Removed the space in `linkedin.com/in/Keith Shannons` (`linkedin.com/in/keith-shannons`) and replaced placeholder domains (`reallygreatsite.com`) with realistic contact details.
* **Missing Summary & Passive Internship Verbs:** Added a concise 2-sentence professional summary at the top and rewrote passive phrasing (*"Learned about the operations..."*) using active contribution verbs (*"Collaborated with the software engineering team to build, test, and debug..."*).
* **Inconsistent Formatting & Typos:** Standardized bullet points across all entries, unified date formats (`Mar 2022 – Aug 2025`, `Oct 2021 – Dec 2021`), fixed capitalization (`JavaScript`), and added a location to the header.
